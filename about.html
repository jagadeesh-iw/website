Our mission

We didn't want to make water. We wanted to make the bottle disappear, so what's left is just the water, and a carton that goes back into the ground instead of staying in it.

Something as essential as drinking water shouldn't come at the cost of our health or the planet. As awareness grew around plastic pollution and microplastics finding their way into water, and into us, we saw a chance to rethink how water gets packaged in the first place. Impact Water is that rethink: a smarter, more responsible alternative to the plastic bottle, built on the belief that small everyday choices add up to real change.
